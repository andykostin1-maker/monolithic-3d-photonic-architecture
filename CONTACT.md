# Contact Guidance

Public inquiries in this repository must remain non-confidential and non-enabling.

Do **not** post confidential implementation details, raw simulator inputs, calibration data, device parameters, layouts, process information, manufacturing know-how, or other non-public technical materials in issues, pull requests, discussions, or comments.

For commercial inquiries or technical due-diligence, collaboration, licensing, acquisition, or investment requests, ask the repository owner to arrange a private contact channel. Sharing any non-public information may require prior written permission and a separate confidentiality agreement.
